# std/sql against Go: row decoding (2026-11-04)

Two measurements, 1,000 rows of five columns (id, name, score, flag, count)
read whole 300 times and summed (checksums equal).

## Postgres driver against pgx v5 (darwin/arm64, Docker, not a quiet window)

bench/perf/sqlpg (by hand: needs PG_URL; its own postgres:17-alpine
container, published on 127.0.0.1:55432 only, started and stopped by
container ID, removed afterwards). Wall seconds of 7 runs each, query_as
against Rows.Scan:

| | runs | median |
|---|---|---|
| Go (pgx v5) | 0.208 0.184 0.187 0.192 0.193 0.187 0.180 | 0.187 |
| Xo | 0.231 0.235 0.233 0.226 0.230 0.237 0.232 | 0.232 |

Xo / Go = 1.24 (the ranges do not overlap). The wall time holds the process
start, the connection and the server's work, the same on both sides.
