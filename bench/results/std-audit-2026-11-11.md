# Std function level audit, third pass, 2026-11-11

Follows std-audit-2026-11-10b.md. Tool: scripts/stdaudit; listings regenerated
on origin/main of 2026-11-11 (after the std/url, std/html, std/htmltok and
std/htmlent merges and ADRs 0587 and 0595).

Commands: `go run ./scripts/stdaudit -go > go.txt`; `-xo bin/xo > xo.txt`;
`-report -go-file go.txt -xo-file xo.txt -map scripts/stdaudit/map.txt > rep.txt`;
`-rank rep.txt -status gap` over the pinned modules of scripts/stdaudit/corpus.txt
(module cache, fixed versions) and, separately, over GOROOT/src (go1.26.1) without cmd.

## What changed in the tool

- The Xo side lists the public surface only (`xo symbols --pub`): a merged
  module's private helpers (std/mail's decode_word, std/binary's le) had
  counted as surface; 4,017 symbols became 3,449, and 9 rows named only by a
  private doc comment fell back to review.
- The type constants of decision 0587 (`Int.max`, `Float.inf`, `Rune.max`),
  which `xo doc TYPE` does not list, are read from std/prelude.xo's
  `@static Type.name` doc comments: the 20 stale rows of math and unicode
  constants resolved.
