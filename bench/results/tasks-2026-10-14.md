(decision 0212 stage 3, under decision 0229's rules). Branch b18-waits.

## What moved

- Stage 1 (commit 484b7205):
  - the task's end (task_done, task_unwound);
  - `t.await`, with the wait list's add, delete, and wait;
  - the task's result, the scope's failed task and site, `s.group()`;
  - fault_rethrow.
  - The wait list's entry is the context's own (new xo_ctx field
    `went`, ctx.xo C_WENT 216; xo_ctx is now 256 bytes), as decision
    0212 stage 3 planned: the C's wl_wait_as uses it too, so a waiter's
    entry no longer lives on its C stack.
  - The C's services Xo needs are rt.sched_rt ops: 17 a context's wait
    (park_wait: it flushes the pending work, helps on a root thread,
    and tells ThreadSanitizer), 18 the empty list.
- Stage 2 (commit af81cee8): `s.cancel()` and the program's task group
  (`os.tasks`, its word rt.static_word 17 in ctx.xo's range);
  rt.sched_rt 19 releases a handle.
- Generated code calls the exports (`xo_x_task_await`, ...) directly on
  arm64 and LLVM. The LLVM emitter takes a pointer result as an Int and
  converts it with inttoptr. The C entry points forward.
- xo_rt.c: 158 lines deleted, 71 added (forwarders and asserts);
  tasks.xo is 315 lines with comments.

## What stays C, and why

- **The spawn** (xo_task_spawn), **the scope's open and close**
  (xo_scope_open, xo_scope_close), **the run of a task**
  (run_claimed), **scope_free and task_free**:
  - a task and a scope are objects whose header names a C free function
    (h_init(t, task_free)), freed through xo_h_free with the real time
    sections' deferral;
  - the spawn initializes the pool (pthread_once), finds or makes the
    root thread's worker, and tells ThreadSanitizer (TSAN_RELEASE);
  - the close deletes the deadline's timer (the poller, B22), flushes
    the pending work, and tells ThreadSanitizer;
  - the run swaps the current context on the C stack and calls the
    entry function through its pointer.
  - These move with B20 (the pool's setup, the worker records, the TSan
    hooks) and B22 (timers), or with Xo handle objects (B13's rest),
    which give the free functions an Xo home.
- **The C wait list functions** (wl_add, wl_del, wl_wake, wl_wait_as)
  stay for the channels', selects', and Mutexes' C (B19). A call from
  that C into Xo on every wait costs what held_check showed in B17.
