# ChaCha20-Poly1305 against Go's chacha20poly1305, 2026-10-22

Program: bench/perf/chacha/main.xo (20,000 records of 16 KiB, 328 MB:
`seal`, `open`, ChaCha20 alone `xor`, Poly1305 alone `poly`) through
internal.crypto_op. Go twin: bench/perf/chacha/twin.go.txt
(golang.org/x/crypto v0.57.0 chacha20poly1305, chacha20, poly1305).
Main bb16421fb (the "before"), native arm64.

Why: TLS 1.2 ChaCha20-Poly1305 bulk was 0.71 to 0.76 of Go
(net-vs-go-2026-10-22.md), server CPU per MB 1.3x Go's. Split, linux/arm64
in Docker, quiet enough for three identical runs (seconds, user time):

| | Xo before | Xo after | Go | after / Go |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| seal | 0.337 | 0.292 | 0.258 | 1.13 |
| poly1305 | 0.124 | 0.072 | 0.079 | 0.91 |
| chacha20 xor | 0.212 | 0.217 | 0.177 | 1.23 |

Cause: Poly1305 had the same instruction count as Go's (1,412 M against
1,375 M on the Mac) but ran 1.5x slower: one block at a time, the five
limb sums, their carries and the next block's multiply are one dependent
chain of about 19 cycles a block (Go's 64 bit limbs: 12). Fix: two blocks
per step, h = (h + m0) r^2 + m1 r, so the two products are independent;
r^2 is computed once in `poly_new`. Instructions go up 1.6% (1,435 M).

`xo bench --ab --rounds=10` on the Mac (B / A, 95% interval), A = main,
B = this change:

| workload | wall | cpu | instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| seal | 0.836 (0.832 to 0.851) | 0.833 | 1.008 |
| open | 0.844 (0.834 to 0.852) | 0.839 | 1.007 |
| poly1305 | 0.571 (0.570 to 0.572) | 0.500 | 1.016 |

Go row, same harness (B = Xo after, A = Go): seal wall 1.025 (1.015 to
1.034), poly1305 wall 0.891 (0.879 to 0.896).

End to end, `bench/perf/tlsrate` bulk TLS 1.2 ChaCha20-Poly1305 (256 MiB,
Docker linux/arm64, load average about 10, so absolute rates are lower
than the quiet window's): Go 1,016 to 1,064 MB/s (medians over 4
interleaved runs), Xo 871 to 880 MB/s, 0.82 to 0.86 of Go (0.73 before
this change in a quieter run: 772 against 1,064).

Still above Go: ChaCha20's NEON kernel (cpu.chacha_blocks4, decision
0329), 1.2x Go's chacha_arm64.s. Handed to Xo Lang 1 (the 8 bit rotate by
tbl, fewer state moves, an 8 block variant); `bench/perf/chacha` `xor` is
the driver.

## The kernel as one loop (Xo Lang 1, 2026-10-22)

The rounds already matched Go's (rev32, tbl, shl and sri); the cost was a
call per 256 bytes with its counters stored first. cpu.chacha_stream4
loops inside the kernel (decision 0329 addendum). Docker linux/arm64
(`scripts/docker-linux.sh arm64`, `xo bench --ab --no-guard
--rounds=10`, B / A with the 95% interval), main ea5a9c087:

| workload | new / main | main / Go | new / Go |
|---|---|---|---|
| xor | 0.888 (0.880 to 0.892) | 1.13 (1.127 to 1.155) | 1.005 (1.002 to 1.008) |
| seal | 0.919 (0.886 to 0.973) | 1.036 (0.973 to 1.112, within noise) | 0.942 (0.876 to 1.061, within noise) |

On the Mac (darwin/arm64) the instructions fall 4.3% (xor) and 2.4%
(seal) and wall time does not change (new / main 0.997, within noise):
main's xor was already even with Go there (new / Go 1.005).
