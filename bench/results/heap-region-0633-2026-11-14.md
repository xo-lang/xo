## The release skipped under decision 0637's workers, and the fix (host 3)

With 0637 stage 3's crawling workers (2a48b9a4b, the default), a preempted
lazy check left region mode on another thread, the count stayed at 1 and
the release did not run (gotchas/region-mode-preempted.md). Cold builds,
`XO_HEAP_REGION_STATS=1`, main 17ea0bf4e against the fix a22959ee2 (region
mode holds off preemption; the workers' lazy checks stay out of the
region), the fix under `XO_LOCKCHECK=1`:

| build | main: release ran | main max RSS | fix: release ran | fix max RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| xo self build, 4 rounds | 3 of 4 | 3.91 to 3.97 GB; 5.52 GB when skipped | 4 of 4 | 3.88 to 3.95 GB |
| aiplatform/apiv1 test build, 3 rounds (serial: no workers) | 3 of 3 | 1.98 to 2.07 GB | 3 of 3 | 2.00 to 2.10 GB |

An earlier run of main on the same host (sr-h3.txt) skipped it in 3 of 4
self builds (5.15 to 5.60 GB) and never with `XO_FE_SPEC=1` (3.80 to
3.96 GB). No lock check report on Linux or on the Mac. The longest window
without preemption is the whole check: 0.64 to 0.87 s for the self build
and 1.07 to 1.97 s for aiplatform on host 3 (0.49 and 0.94 s on the Mac).
The fix pools fewer units (about 67,000 against 336,000) because the
workers' lazy checks now allocate outside the region; max RSS is the same.
