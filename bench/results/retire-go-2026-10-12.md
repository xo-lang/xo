# xo1 against every golden suite, 2026-10-12 (decision 0178)

How much of `xo build` the self hosted driver xo1 (`selfhost/xo`)
replaces today. xo1 is built by the Go compiler with `--backend=arm64`
(A; its assembly is the fixed point's, decision 0174). Apple M4 Pro,
macOS, about ten other agents' work on the machine, so the wall clock
figures are noisy; the counts are what matters. Script:
the scratchpad's measure.sh and measure2.sh, which run the commands
below in order through the shared heavy lock.

## What the suites do not cover

`xo1` is still one directory per build, the host only on the Go
backend, and has none of `--race`, `--holes`, `--no-mono`,
`--no-arenas`, `--release`, `--ci`, `--seed`/`--explore` on the Go
backend's tests, `--record`, the binary cache, split LLVM builds,
`--real` tests, regressions_test.xo, or the check's diagnostics in its
own output (decision 0178 lists them as stage 1).

## Stage 3: the semantic tools (2026-10-12)

Branch worktree-agent-a248e0f9a76109b67 on main a430a1e with cli-core's
branch (stage 2) merged; xo1 built by the Go `xo build selfhost/xo`
(Go backend). Commands ported: `query` (type, def, callers, effects,
holes, api, imports, deps, hash), `edit` (rename, add-field,
change-sig); `doc --json` is stage 2's; internal/release is ported
(release.xo) for stage 4's `publish`.

| check | result |
|---|---|
| TestSelfhostDefhash: definition hashes of selfhost/cli/semantic against internal/defhash, JSON of every definition, every module of bench/, internal/{check,gen,arm64}/testdata, selfhost/, std/, examples/ | **270 of 270 modules (13,662 definitions) byte identical** |
| cmd/xo tests with `XO_CLI=xo1 XO_STD=<repo>/std go test -short` | **3 of 5 pass**: TestQueryCLI, TestEditCLI (6 of 6 cases), TestDocCLI; TestQueryHashAndPublish fails at `xo publish` (stage 4) after its `query hash` steps pass; TestModules fails at `check --json` of a module with requirements (exit 2, want 1), before its query, doc, and edit steps |
| the same command lines run by the Go `xo` and by xo1 in fresh copies of the cmd/xo test programs (toolsProg, editProg): stdout, stderr, exit status, and every file afterwards | **37 of 37 byte identical** |

The 37 command lines: every `query` of TestQueryCLI with `--json` and
`--text`, `def` of a field and a type, `effects` of an inferred
function, `type` of a binding and of a selector; `doc`, `doc --json`,
`doc --all --json`; the six TestEditCLI cases, change-sig renaming a
parameter, add-field with and without a default; `query hash` as JSON,
text, and a misspelled name.

Without `XO_STD`, TestDocCLI fails in xo1 (stage 2's doc finds std only
through `--std`, `XO_STD`, or lib/xo/std; the semantic tools also look
for the repository's std/ above the working directory).

Found on the way: selfhost/check cli.xo `mod_root_dir` looped forever
when no xo.mod is at or above the directory (`parent("/a")` is "", and
`parent` climbs from "" to "." to "../.." without end); it uses
`parent_dir` now.
