## Litmus tests (bench/litmus)

Two tasks meet at a barrier before each iteration; sums of 20 runs of
n = 200,000 iterations per test. "weak" counts the outcome the stronger
orders forbid (SB: r1 == r2 == 0; MP: flag seen, data not); the counter
tests (CAS loop, relaxed fetch add, swap lock over relaxed accesses,
Atomic[Int].add; 4 tasks) count runs with a lost update.

The relaxed columns are the positive controls: the harness does see
the weak outcomes (MP's never appears on x86, whose stores and loads
keep their order). Linux arm64 read modify writes are calls of the
outline atomics (libgcc's, which use LSE on Graviton). Instances:
i-EXAMPLE17 (arm64), i-EXAMPLE52 (amd64), on demand,
terminated; a first pair (i-EXAMPLE35, i-EXAMPLE15) ran
bb270dd6, whose `offsetof` did not compile with glibc's headers.
xo1 built in 585 s on the Graviton.
