# The predicate forms of Str: Xo against Go, 2026-10-23

Program: `bench/perf/unicode` with four new workloads, beside its Go twin
`bench/perf/unicode/go` doing the same work with `strings.IndexFunc`,
`strings.TrimFunc`, `strings.FieldsFunc` and `strings.Map`. Instructions
retired (`/usr/bin/time -l`, best of 5), arm64 native and an LLVM -O1
build of the same program. The machine was loaded (the lead's hourly gate
ran alongside), so wall time is noise and the instruction counts decide.

Every workload's checksum is identical on arm64, llvm and Go: indexfunc
114800200, trimfunc 23476600, fieldsfunc 4640000, mapfunc 953254865.

| workload | arm64 | llvm | Go | arm64 / Go | llvm / Go |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| indexfunc (find_where, full scan) | 7,448 M | 7,204 M | 9,856 M | 0.76 | 0.73 |
| trimfunc (trim_where, 4,000 spaces) | 65.8 M | 50.1 M | 104.5 M | 0.63 | 0.48 |
| fieldsfunc (split_where, ASCII words) | 251 M | 201 M | 394 M | 0.64 | 0.51 |
| mapfunc (map_runes, rot13 over mixed) | 5,938 M | 6,785 M | 4,929 M | 1.20 | 1.38 |

Wall time (best us, the loaded machine): indexfunc 300,701 / 303,883 /
367,765, trimfunc 2,285 / 1,227 / 2,865, fieldsfunc 14,182 / 7,828 /
17,359, mapfunc 273,225 / 310,750 / 203,648 (arm64 / llvm / Go).

What it says:

- The scans (find_where, trim_where, split_where) run at 0.5 to 0.8 of
  Go's instructions. The ASCII arm decodes nothing, split_where records
  spans in one pass and slices the pieces afterwards, and the trims
  return views, which is Go's own shape for the first two and cheaper
  than Go's copy for the third.
- map_runes is 1.2 to 1.4 times Go's instructions: Go's `strings.Map`
  writes into a `strings.Builder` sized once, while this one appends a
  rune at a time through `raw.push_rune` into a native string buffer.
  The checksum and the once-per-rune call order match Go; the gap is the
  builder, not the decoding. The profile agreed and the append path was
  fixed on 2026-10-24: 4,240 M on arm64 and 4,485 M on LLVM against Go's
  4,958 M, 0.86 and 0.90 of Go's instructions
  (`maprunes-batch-2026-10-24.md`).

Reproduce:

```
bench/perf/unicode/measure.sh "$PWD/bin/xo" /tmp/u arm64 llvm
bench/perf/unicode/measurego.sh /tmp/u
```
