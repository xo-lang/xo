# std/yaml against gopkg.in/yaml.v3 (2026-11-05)

Decision 0512. `bench/perf/yamlderive`: a Kubernetes style Deployment (about 600 bytes, nested
structs, lists of structs, a Map) decoded 20,000 times into a struct that derives Yaml
(`decode`), and the decoded value encoded 20,000 times (`encode`); the Go twin is
`twin.go.txt` (yaml.v3 v3.0.1, vendored in `govendor`) with `yaml.Unmarshal` and `yaml.Marshal`
into the same struct. Host: linux/arm64 offload host, `xo bench --ab` style twin rows
(`--release`), 7 rounds; both sides print the same checksum.

| workload | Go ms | Xo ms | Xo/Go | range | rss Xo/Go |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| decode | 714.6 | 443.2 | 0.62 | 0.607-0.639 | 0.15 |
| encode | 276.7 | 167.1 | 0.604 | 0.595-0.618 | 0.162 |

Rows below 1 mean Xo is faster. Both are with the alias expansion accounting and the type
errors collected (the readers of the 2026-11-05 differential, `testdata/corpus/yaml`).
The decode row without them measured 0.584 (the first prototype) and 0.612 (collecting
readers), so the accounting costs under 2%.

Correctness of the same code: 442 documents and 8,000 seeded random documents decode to
yaml.v3's value or error text, and the encoder writes `yaml.Marshal`'s bytes
(testdata/corpus/yaml, yamlfuzz, `TestYamlFuzz`).
