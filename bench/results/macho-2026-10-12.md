# The self hosted Mach-O assembler and linker (2026-10-12)

Decision 0182. `selfhost/macho`, in Xo, assembles the arm64 emitter's
assembly into Mach-O objects (stage 1) and links them into executables
(stage 2), so xo1 needs no `cc`.

## Setup

- Host: darwin/arm64, macOS 27.0, Apple clang 21.0.0
  (clang-2100.1.1.101), `GOMAXPROCS=4`. About ten agents shared the
  machine, so wall times are noisy; counts are exact.
- The assembler: `selfhost/macho` built by the Go `xo` with
  `--backend=arm64` (`xo build --backend=arm64 -o macho ./macho` in
  `selfhost/`).
- Inputs: the assembly of every arm64 golden (`xo asmdump` of the 133
  `internal/llvm/testdata/golden` and 25 `internal/arm64/testdata/run`
  programs, 158 files, 1,212,359 lines) and of xo1 itself (`xo asmdump
  selfhost/xo`, 13,759,561 lines, 258 MB).
- Comparison: `scripts/macho-compare.py <macho> <files.s>` assembles
  each file with `macho asm --minos=27.0` and `cc -c` (clang -cc1as),
  and compares whole files, each section's bytes, each function's
  `__text` bytes (between consecutive symbols), and the relocation
  tables with symbol numbers replaced by names.

## Stage 1: the assembler

| Input | Objects identical | Functions identical | Sections identical | Relocation tables identical | Object bytes (macho / cc) | Assembly time (macho / cc) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 158 arm64 goldens | 38 of 158 | 3,792 of 3,792 | 904 of 904 | 904 of 904 | 6,596,328 / 6,596,328 | 1.45 s / 9.93 s (all files) |
| xo1 (selfhost/xo) | 0 of 1 | 2,825 of 2,825 | 6 of 6 | 6 of 6 | 57,025,520 / 57,025,520 | 4.92 s / 35.40 s |

Every section (`__text`, `__gcc_except_tab`, `__const`, `__cstring`,
`__compact_unwind`, `__eh_frame`) and every relocation is identical to
the system assembler's on every input. The 120 objects that are not
byte identical differ only in the order of the local symbols in the
symbol table (and so in the symbol numbers of relocations and the
string table offsets): LLVM registers a symbol an instruction names at
a point this assembler does not reproduce yet (a function defined before
its first call can come after it). Peak memory on xo1's assembly: 1.01
GB (macho) and 0.98 GB (cc).
