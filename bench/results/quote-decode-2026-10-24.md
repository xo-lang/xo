# Quote: the two byte rune inline and a printable bitmap, 10% fewer instructions, 2026-10-24

`unicode.quote` walks a Str or Bytes: `scan.span` skips the runs of
bytes that go over as they are, and at every other byte it decodes the
rune and asks `is_print`. The profile of `bench/perf/unicode`'s quote
workload (a 240 KB text of ASCII and Greek, Cyrillic, accented Latin,
Japanese, halfwidth katakana and astral letters, with `\t`, `\a` and
`\0` every 200 bytes) put three things above 20 percent:

| function | flat | cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| raw.decode | 22.2% | 22.2% |
| scan.span (stops8, eq8, addw) | 14.5% | 32.1% |
| is_print | 6.0% | 21.3% |
| in_ranges | 15.0% | under is_print |
| quote itself | 16.2% | 95.4% |

Profile: 414 samples, 4,140 ms of a 4,431 ms run, at 100 Hz
(`XO_PROF=cpu`, `xo prof`).

## The two changes

**std/native/llrt/unicode.xo `decode2`**: the two byte sequence, which
is the whole of Greek, Cyrillic and accented Latin text, answered
without calling `raw.decode`: a lead byte in 0xC2 to 0xDF (0xC0 and 0xC1
are the overlong forms) and a continuation byte in 0x80 to 0xBF give
`((c & 0x1F) << 6) | (c1 & 0x3F)` of size 2, and a bad or missing
continuation gives U+FFFD of size 1, both as raw.xo's decode rules say.
3 and 4 byte sequences still call `raw.decode`. `quote` uses it;
`case_append` already had its own copy of this case.

**PRINT_BMP**: `is_print` for the runes U+0800 to U+FFFF from a bitmap
(7,936 bytes) instead of the ranges search, which it keeps for the
astral planes. The table is generated by internal/llvm
TestUnicodeTables with the other Unicode tables.

## A/B, one revision, A = base, B = the change

`xo bench --ab --rounds=10 --metrics=instructions`, the same
bench/perf/unicode source built by each xo. B/A below 1.0 is better.
The machines were loaded (load average 10.8), so instructions are
reported and wall is not.

| workload | A | B | B/A | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| quote, decode2 | 108,438,550 | 102,075,466 | 0.941 | -5.9% |
| classes, decode2 (control) | 446,012,037 | 441,715,381 | 0.990 | code layout |
| quote, PRINT_BMP | 101,731,788 | 97,902,513 | 0.962 | -3.8% |
| classes, PRINT_BMP | 441,405,452 | 434,234,468 | 0.984 | classify asks is_print |
| lint, PRINT_BMP (control) | 28,702,766 | 28,669,228 | 0.999 | within noise |

Against the base of both (`a0bb9076a`, the rune class index), the quote
row goes 108,438,550 to 97,902,513, 0.903. The quote checksum is
628,880,865 on all three builds, and classes' 129,070,000.

## What the bitmap costs a program

A program that calls `is_print` (the quote path) carries the 7,936 byte
table: `bench/perf/unicode`'s `__TEXT,__const` grows 127,553 to 135,513
bytes and its file does not grow (the segment is page padded).

## Coverage

TestUnicodeTables regenerates and compares the table file; the `runes`,
`json`, `jsonencode`, `jsondecode`, `strings`, `strconv`, `template`
and `csv` corpora on arm64 and llvm, the wasm goldens (TestSelfhostWasm)
and the full fixed point, since these are std/native files.
