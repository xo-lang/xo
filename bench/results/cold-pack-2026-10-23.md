# A cold build's cache entries as one shared pack segment (2026-10-23)

Lead-approved item (A) of the race-pack design note: a cold build wrote
each new cache entry as its own file (about 5,800 for a small program:
the bodies of std and the runtime it uses), which a Windows runner writes
slowly. compiler/xo bcache.xo store_entries now writes a build's new
entries of a kind as one segment of the shared pack (decision 0351) when
there are COLD_PACK_MIN (64) or more; every reader looks there before the
loose files. XO_COLD_PACK=off writes them loose as before. The parts of a
split program stay in their target's pack.

Development Mac (Apple M4 Pro, 24 GB), xo from this commit's tree, program
internal/llvm/testdata/golden/conc_mutex as a one-file module.

## Files and bytes after one cold build

| XO_COLD_PACK | files in the cache | size |
|---|---|---|
| off | 6,227 | 36 MB |
| on (default) | 638 | 20 MB |

The warm build after it: the same reuse with both (`cache fe 1252/1257
bodies`, `cache functions 614/614 reused`; loose fe reads 1,252 with off,
0 with on, all from the shared pack), and its binary identical to the cold
build's; the binaries of the two settings are identical.

## The shared pack's growth across xo versions (2026-10-23, later)

After the lead's Windows report (windows-race.sh on main took 44 minutes
against the 0.27.0 archive's 323 s). On the Mac (xo at cbfa9b100, gate
class), the script's four builds (plain tsan_race, and tsan_race,
race_sync and race_stacks with --race, all --backend=amd64
--targets=windows/amd64), a new cache each mode, then the same again:

| XO_COLD_PACK | first build | after 4 builds | after 8 builds |
|---|---|---|---|
| on (default) | 5.65 s, 19 files, 26 MB | 125 files, 47.7 MB | 132 files, 69 MB; shared 39 MB in 11 segments |
| off | 8.98 s, 10,185 files, 56 MB | 13,765 files, 112.8 MB | 13,777 files, 146 MB; shared 39 MB in 12 segments |

Each later build took 4.6 to 6.1 s either way: on the Mac the cold pack
is not slower and writes far less.

What does grow: a cold build of new keys (a new xo, here XO_INLINE_DENY
of a function no program has, eight values, the same program each time):

| generation | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| on, before the fix: shared pack MB | 26 | 39 | 51 | 64 | 77 | 89 | 102 | 114 |
| off: shared pack MB | 0 | 13.5 | 13.5 | 13.7 | 13.7 | 13.7 | 13.7 | 13.7 |
| on, with the fix: shared pack MB | 26 | 39 | 51 | 26 | 39 | 51 | 26 | 39 |

Build times stayed at 4.8 to 6.3 s in every column on the Mac.

The race binaries themselves are not slower: built by the 0.27.0 archive
and by main (darwin/amd64 --race, the archive's ThreadSanitizer dylib)
and timed on the Intel laptop, one pass of the script's 26 runs took
57.8 s against 59.2 s, every case within noise of the other (exit -6,
an abort, on darwin where windows exits 66, the same for both).
