What each version changed:

- **v1**: the base's IR manifest may be an
  ancestor's within 30 commits (a superset of the base's selection), so a
  gate builds only HEAD's; scripts/gate-areas.txt scopes the slow tests of
  internal/llvm, internal/arm64 and cmd/xo to the paths that can break
  them (go test -skip); TestModuleCheckAuto left the arm64 step (the
  chkcache step and the tier run it).
- **v2**: the parents of parallel subtests in internal/arm64 scoped
  (TestDebugInfoSameCode, TestNoLeafWithPads, TestIfaceLexMatchesParse,
  TestGoldenPassesOff, TestWindowsAmd64Identity), TestSupplyChainAttacks
  and TestRepl in cmd/xo; HEAD's IR manifest built with a third of the
  CPUs (6 on host 2) where it used 4.
- **v3**: the llvm package's tests that do not
  read the selection run while it is made, and TestGoldenTasks and
  TestGoldenASan follow the selection outside the runtime, scheduler,
  race, HTTP, LLVM backend and driver areas. In after2.tsv they built
  every task and network golden with no golden selected: 2,610 and
  1,251 s of subtest time.

## Correction: the ancestor manifest (2026-11-15)

v1 let the newest cached manifest within 30 commits of the base stand in
for the base's own: the goldens it selects are the base's plus those the
commits between changed. That is a superset, so nothing is lost, but it
is only small when those commits change little IR. On this main nearly
every window has compiler commits, and those change most goldens' IR:
in 14 consecutive pairs of host 3's cached manifests, the windows with
compiler commits differed in 647 to 719 of about 720 goldens, a window
with only std/native/json and llrt changes in 124, and two windows in 0.

The same comment change, gated on host 1 (linux/arm64, 12 CPUs, 40 GB,
the timing host, nothing else running), steps repo, arm64, llvm and cmd
at once ([h1-before.tsv](gate-time-2026-11-14/h1-before.tsv),
[h1-now.tsv](gate-time-2026-11-14/h1-now.tsv)). Neither run includes
0b0a672f7 (selfbuild under XO_LOCKCHECK), and neither ran a selfbuild
step:

| | Before (bc41bfef9) | Now (8db04e454, main 8021fb113) |
|---|---:|---:|
| llvm: golden selection | 1,956 | 1,415 |
| llvm: goldens selected | 0 of 723 | 722 of 728 |
| llvm step | 3,250 | 3,437 |
| arm64 step | 1,778 | 1,749 |
| cmd step | 1,415 | 1,074 |
| steps took (wall) | 3,322 | 3,509 |
| host memory peak | 22,286 MB | 17,065 MB |

"Now" took 3,509 s against 3,322 s. Its only cached manifest was
7fee0c873's, 25 commits before the base and across compiler commits, so
it selected 722 of 728 goldens and ran nearly all of them, in llvm and,
through the arm64 selection, in arm64. The cmd step's scoping (v1, v2 and
TestHabitGolden below) held: 1,415 to 1,074 s.

The fixes: an ancestor's manifest stands in only when the commits from
it to the base change no IR input (61ff68d4a, gate.sh
ir_inputs_changed; on the 14 pairs above it called every pair whose IR
differed changed), else the base's own is built, at ir_jobs' job count
where it used 4; and scripts/lead/irman.sh makes main's manifest once
per main commit that changes an IR input, on host 3, and copies it to
every arm64 host, so a gate finds its base's manifest instead of
building it.

## Hidden time: parents of parallel subtests

go test -json's Elapsed for a parent leaves out its parallel subtests.
Summed subtest time in the lead's tier 915 arm64 run: TestDebugInfoSameCode
5,290 s, TestNoLeafWithPads 2,951 s, TestIfaceLexMatchesParse 2,100 s,
TestModuleCheckAuto 1,226 s, TestFrontCacheAcrossPrograms 1,132 s,
TestGoldenPassesOff 572 s, TestWindowsAmd64Identity 304 s. gate.sh's
times.tsv records each parent's subtest sum (kind `subtests`) since v2.

## What still runs for this change (after2.tsv), and why

A compiler/ssa change runs the arm64 tests that a pass can break:
TestGoldenRun (3,523 s of subtests), TestFrontCacheAcrossPrograms
(2,048 s, the front end cache) and TestGoldenPassesOff (686 s). These
are the gate's coverage, not its floor. The arm64 step has no golden
selection like llvm's; that is the next step for it.

## Targets

The plan's targets were llvm at most 12 min, arm64 at most 10 min, and a
typical compiler gate at most 25 min of wall time. On host 1 with today's
main: llvm 57 min, arm64 29 min, wall 58 min for a comment change, all
of it from the stale manifest above. The base's own manifest (irman.sh)
is the change that decides whether the targets are met; its measurement
follows here once it is on main.

## The daily check

scripts/lead/gatetime.sh, run once a day by hourly.sh, reads every gate's
times.tsv from the last 24 hours against
scripts/lead/gatetime-baseline.txt and logs regressions to the lead's
test-time.log and bottleneck.log: a step median over its baseline by more
than 25%, an llvm step over 900 s with 5 or fewer goldens selected, a
top-level test of 60 s or more or a parent with 600 s or more of subtests
that gate-areas.txt does not scope. Tested on 186 gates of the Mac's logs
(2026-11-14): it flagged civ2's llvm floor.
