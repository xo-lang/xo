# Borrowed projections (decision 0186), 2026-10-12

rc-borrow agent. `before` is the compiler at d819a82 (main before
decision 0186, an archive copy); `after` is this branch (d468930 and
later). Apple M4 Pro shared by about ten
agents (load averages 6 to 22): wall clock is noisy, counts are exact.

Retain and release counts are the operations an arm64 build executes:
measured with scratch copies of both compilers that call a counter in
the runtime before every `retain` and `release` the arm64 emitter emits
(after its own cancellation), printed at exit with `XO_LEAKCHECK=1`.
The instrumentation (scratchpad `instrument.py`) is not committed; the
timed binaries are built without it.

## bench/perf/nested

`var` receiver, 20,000 four field instructions, 10 passes) and `index2`
(`a[i][j]` over 100 rows of 200, 10 passes). Times are the median of
three runs of the program's own clock (microseconds); checksums are the
same before and after on both backends, and allocations are the same on
both.

## The golden borrow_proj, case by case (arm64)

Each case alone (a scratch copy of the golden's functions, `mk(5)` and
one call; the baseline of `mk` and `main` alone is 41 allocations, 1
retain, 19 releases):

| case | allocations before | after | retains before | after | releases before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| count (reads only) | 41 | 41 | 17 | 1 | 35 | 19 |
| count_logging (`var` call in the body) | 42 | 42 | 12 | 7 | 25 | 20 |
| grow (push to the walked list) | 53 | 53 | 18 | 12 | 26 | 20 |
| bump_all (nested update, 0169) | 47 | 47 | 7 | 2 | 25 | 20 |
| sum_into (compound assignment, 0159) | 42 | 42 | 7 | 7 | 20 | 20 |
| pick (element pushed, one returned) | 42 | 42 | 20 | 13 | 30 | 23 |
| find (break with the element live) | 41 | 41 | 14 | 13 | 32 | 31 |
| rows (`a[i][j]` read, then `a[i][0] =`) | 51 | 51 | 14 | 1 | 33 | 20 |
| sum_rows (`var` list of lists) | 48 | 48 | 11 | 1 | 30 | 20 |
| shapes (payload of an element) | 43 | 43 | 5 | 1 | 24 | 20 |
| last_long (element escapes the loop) | 44 | 44 | 7 | 7 | 27 | 27 |

The first version (without rule 3) made last_long's caller 3
allocations dearer (47): gotchas/borrowed-projection-keeps-owner-alive.md.
In count_logging (checked in its rc stage) the body's `var` call
consumes `g`, so the block projection `g.blocks[b]` keeps its count, and
the element is borrowed from that block's list, which nothing in the
loop consumes.
