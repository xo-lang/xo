`bench/perf/http/run.sh` (task 01 users CRUD API, loadgen's create, get,
patch, get, delete cycle with a list every eighth cycle), 32 workers,
5 s measured after 1 s warmup, medians of 3 interleaved runs, loopback.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro (12 cores), darwin/arm64, Apple clang 21, Go
1.26.1; load average 2 to 12 during the runs (another session's work).
Xo built from this branch (`XO=<scratch xo>`). Config `xo` is the Go
backend built with `--no-arenas`, `xo-llvm` and `xo-arm64` the native

## Reading

- Over one HTTP/2 connection the native servers do 1.5 times the Go
  backend's requests at 73 percent of its CPU per request (37 against
  51 us), with no errors. net/http's HTTP/2 server costs Go 30 percent
  more CPU per request than its HTTP/1.1 server here (51 against 39
  us); the native HTTP/2 server costs 8 percent more than the native
  HTTP/1.1 one (37 against 34 us).
- One connection caps every configuration below its HTTP/1.1 rate with
  32 connections: a connection's frames go through one serving loop on
  both backends (net/http's `serverConn.serve` goroutine, the native
  connection's fiber) and through one TLS stream, and loadgen's client
  side is one connection too (its read loop and write lock). The p50 of
  about 350 us with 32 streams in flight is the native rate itself
  (32 / 350 us is about 91k/s).
- Memory: native peak RSS 24 MB against 46 MB on the Go backend.

## Next steps (ranked by likely gain)

1. Measure where a native HTTP/2 request's 37 us go: `sample <pid> 4`
   on `xo-llvm` under `H2=1` (the encoder's linear search of the static
   table per field, a header list copy per response, and one
   `mbedtls_ssl_write` per batch are the suspects); compare with
   `H2=1 CONC=128` to see whether the single serving fiber saturates.
2. Several connections: loadgen `-h2` with a `-conns N` option (one
   Transport per group of workers) to measure HTTP/2 the way browsers
   and proxies load a server (6 to 100 connections), where the native
   server's per connection fibers run in parallel.
3. A multiplexing native client (decision 0121, alternatives): requests
   at once share a connection as Go's do; measure with a native load
   generator in Xo against both servers.
