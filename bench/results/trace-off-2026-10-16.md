median of 11 interleaved runs. bench/perf/conc/trace-off.sh builds each
workload with one xo (the change's, with the cbz isel) and main's std
(no hooks) against the change's std (hooks, trace off).

between runs (scheduling); the one-thread figures are steady. What the

checks down: a context word for the hot hooks (a load of a record in a
register, not of the static word: 1.017 to 1.012) and the arm64 isel's
cbz for a compare with 0.
