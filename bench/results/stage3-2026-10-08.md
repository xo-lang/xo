## Design gap found (spec 0.7 candidate)

Graceful drain under SIGTERM. Core 7.5 makes the first signal a sticky
cancellation of main's root scope, so "stop reading, finish what is queued,
flush, exit 0" (task 10) is only possible by doing the drain inside shielded
`defer` blocks, which the subject called legal but odd. Proposal: a two phase
shutdown. The first signal sets a "stop requested" state that tasks observe
(`task.stop_requested() -> Bool`, or `os.signals()` as a channel) without
cancelling anything; servers stop accepting; after a grace period (default
30s) or a second signal, the root scope is cancelled. `FakeProc.signal_after`
would trigger the first phase.

The subject also could not use `signal_after` against a fake stdin that is
fully available at once, and wrote a function type with `uses fx` followed by
a comma in a parameter list as a type alias to avoid the ambiguity (core 15
requires parentheses there).
