## Reading

- Off, the single threaded paths are unchanged (alloc_fast and the
  solo heap never see the profile): 0.996 to 1.009. A threaded
  allocation and free each load the heap record's H_PROF word: tasks
  1.023, conc spawn 1.028 instructions, the rest within 1%.
- Two shapes cost more on the way and were fixed: a call in hfree that
  was not last gave every free a frame (tasks 1.061 off,
  gotchas/arm64-call-anywhere-costs-frame.md), and calling the hook on
  every free (a set probe) instead of only on a marked block's, with
  the countdown out of line (tasks 1.50 on).
- On: a threaded program pays 1.08 for the hooks and 1.01 for the
  samples (tasks). A single threaded one pays the threaded heap the
  profile switches it to (a thread local read per allocation on macOS,
  the magazine paths): 1.32 to 1.98 before any sample. The samples cost
  1% to 7% (mixed, 1,089 samples: the unwind walk about 1.5 us each,
  first touches of the table's pages).
- The writer symbolizes each distinct pc once (it did so per frame per
  sample first: 58% of a 29,273 sample run's time).
- Next: let a single threaded program keep its solo heap while
  profiling (a countdown in alloc_fast's counting branch, which is off
  unless counting, or a profile bit in the heap word), which would take
  the 1.32 to 1.98 away.

## The armed bit (2026-10-16, after a17622af's tbz and 80c9d608)

The profile's off check moved from a load of the heap record's H_PROF
word to bit 1 of the thread's heap word (obj.xo ARMED), which halloc and
hfree already hold in a register. Same xo (main 80c9d608), three stds:
none (the hooks removed), bit (this change), main (the H_PROF load);
