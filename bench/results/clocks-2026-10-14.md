# The clocks in Xo on every OS (work item B01)

Date: 2026-10-14. Work item B01 of
`bench/results/zero-c-audit-refresh-2026-10-13.md` (decision 0213 item
6, decision 0180's bar: within 5 percent of the C path's instructions on
arm64 and LLVM).

## What moved

- std/native/llrt/sysio.xo: `clock_now` (Clock.now), `real_ns`, and
  `mono_ns`. The clock of the build is chosen once into word 0 of
  rt.sys_words (on Linux the vDSO's clock_gettime or -1 for the system
  call, as before; -2 libSystem; -3 kernel32), so the hot paths test no
  `target` (gotchas/arm64-match-target-not-folded.md). macOS calls
  clock_gettime_nsec_np inline (CLOCK_REALTIME, CLOCK_MONOTONIC);
  Windows calls windows.clock_ns (GetSystemTimePreciseAsFileTime) and
  windows.mono_ns (QueryPerformanceCounter, the frequency kept in word 2
  of rt.sys_words); Linux's monotonic clock calls the vDSO inline with
  its timespec in words 5 and 6 of the thread's runtime table
  (rt.tlocal_addr).
- The test world's virtual clock stays the C runtime's: rt.sys_clock_buf
  answers 0 while it runs, now on every OS (on macOS it answers 1
  otherwise, not an address: gotchas/darwin-thread-local-address-costs-a-call.md).
- cabi.xo exports `real_ns` and `mono_ns`; the C runtime's mono_ns (the
  timers, budgets, shields, the scheduler's C paths, xo_http.c and
  xo_h2.c), xo_clock_now's real time, and xo_tz_now call them. pool.xo
  reads sysio.mono_ns directly (its `match layer()` and the RT_NOW
  service are gone).
- Deleted C: xo_win.c's clock_gettime shim (CLOCK_REALTIME,
  CLOCK_MONOTONIC, xo_clock_gettime, the macro: 27 lines), every
  clock_gettime call of std/llrt outside xo_nolibc.c, and mono_ns's
  body. std/llrt: 46 lines deleted, 19 added. Kept, with their C
  callers: mono_ns and xo_tz_now as one line forwards, xo_clock_now (the
  virtual clock; xo_http.c's Date and xo_test_now call it),
  xo_sys_clock_buf; xo_nolibc.c's clock_gettime (Linux, XO_NOLIBC=1,
  for its time()).

## Method

bench/perf/sysio/measure.sh with one xo (bin/xo of the branch) building
the same program twice: with the std of main 5ef72e4e (the C clocks:
the C path) and with the branch's std (the Xo clocks). The c variant
(`XO_LLRT_C=sysio`) no longer exists, so the std tree is the switch.
Minimum of 5 runs per row; two rounds (base then branch, twice).
Workloads: clock (500,000 Clock.now), sleep (20,000 `clock.sleep(1us)`:
the timers' monotonic clock), chan (200,000 channel values: pool.xo's
monotonic clock), none. Ten agents shared the Mac; instruction counts
are robust to that, the chan workload's are not (below).
