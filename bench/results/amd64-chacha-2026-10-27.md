# amd64 ChaCha20 and ChaCha20-Poly1305 against Go (2026-10-27)

Gap 3 of [amd64-parity-by-target-2026-10-27.md](amd64-parity-by-target-2026-10-27.md),
after [amd64-crypto-vs-go-2026-10-27.md](amd64-crypto-vs-go-2026-10-27.md)
measured ChaCha20-Poly1305 at 20.39 times Go and ChaCha20 alone at 5.172
times x/crypto's (Go code on amd64).

Machine, method and programs as there: the i7-9750H (SSSE3, AVX2; Xo's x86
encoder has no AVX2 yet), `xo bench --ab --rounds=11`, N=20000 records of
16 KiB, bench/perf/chacha and its Go twin (golang.org/x/crypto v0.57.0,
Go 1.26.1), darwin/amd64 builds cross built on the Mac.

Two steps:

1. The portable block keeps its sixteen words in locals (it made two
   Lists a 64 byte block and indexed them in every quarter round).
2. cpu.chacha_blocks4 and chacha_stream4 lowered with SSSE3 on amd64
   (compiler/ssa x86chacha.xo): Go's horizontal SSE layout, a block's rows
   in four xmm registers, two blocks interleaved, pshufd for the
   diagonals, pshufb for the rotations by 16 and 8.

| workload | Go ms | before | locals | SSSE3 | SSSE3 / Go (95% interval) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ChaCha20 (xor) | 529.4 | 2755 | 676.3 | 257.3 | 0.486 (0.481 to 0.488) |
| ChaCha20-Poly1305 seal | 146.4 | 2950 | 861.2 | 453.7 | 3.098 (3.059 to 3.152) |

The steps' own A/Bs: locals against the parent, xor 0.244 (0.243 to
0.246), seal 0.293 (0.292 to 0.294); SSSE3 against locals, xor 0.382
(0.379 to 0.387), seal 0.519 (0.517 to 0.524).

Correctness: the chacha_blocks4 golden (its want.txt is Go's ChaCha20)
built for darwin/amd64 with the native amd64 backend and with LLVM, run on
the i7-9750H, equals Go's output; the twin's checksums (seal, open, xor,
poly at N=7) equal Go's.

What is left of seal: ChaCha20 257 ms and Poly1305 about 190 ms
(Poly1305 alone 1.788 times Go's), run one after the other; Go's
chacha20poly1305 runs AVX2 ChaCha20 (eight blocks) with Poly1305
interleaved in one loop. Next: AVX2 needs VEX.256 in the x86 encoder
(vpaddd, vpxor, vpshufb, vpslld, vpsrld, vpor, vperm2i128, vmovdqu,
vzeroupper); Poly1305's limb arithmetic is the other half.
