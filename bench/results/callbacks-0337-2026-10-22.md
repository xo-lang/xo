# Callback walks before and after decision 0337 (2026-10-22)

Decision 0337: a function value borrows its arguments, except one of its
result's type (or of a tuple result's). Measured with
bench/perf/callbacks (`measure.sh XO OUT arm64 llvm`): a list of 1000
`User{id, name: Str, email: Str}` walked 20,000 times for a name never
found (20 M element visits), each walk in its own function; the `i`
rows walk users of three Ints; the `two_` rows run the walk on two
tasks over one list shared across threads (40 M visits). Best of 5,
instructions retired from `/usr/bin/time -l`, the development Mac
(arm64), 2026-10-22. Before: xo of origin/main 517e6e03c (the cached
build of 42daa44ed; no compiler or std/native change between them).
After: the same plus 0337. Same std, a private back-end cache.
LLVM at the default -O2 of `xo build --backend=llvm`.

| Workload | arm64 before | arm64 after | change | LLVM before | LLVM after | change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| for | 455 M | 455 M | 0% | 178 M | 178 M | 0% |
| any | 1,759 M | 1,160 M | -34% | 2,066 M | 1,507 M | -27% |
| filter | 1,859 M | 1,258 M | -32% | 2,126 M | 1,568 M | -26% |
| fold | 1,798 M | 1,238 M | -31% | 2,066 M | 1,544 M | -25% |

| iany | 782 M | 782 M | 0% | 1,244 M | 1,244 M | 0% |
| ifold | 822 M | 821 M | 0% | 1,183 M | 1,184 M | 0% |

Best wall time of the shared walks (one run each of 5, microseconds):
two_any arm64 191,262 to 74,603 (-61%), LLVM 314,829 to 83,392 (-74%);
two_fold arm64 199,527 to 76,303, LLVM 401,457 to 68,850. The single
task walks: any arm64 89,252 to 72,788 us.

Read:

- The Int rows do not move: their elements hold no heap memory, so
  nothing was counted before either. They are the cost of the call
  itself.
- After 0337 a string walk through a callback costs its Int walk plus
  the string compare (any arm64: 1,160 M = iany 782 M + the compare,
  about 300 M as for minus ifor): the counts are gone. The two task
  walks lose most: their counts were atomic on shared count lines.
- What remains between `any` and `for` is the call through the function
  value: about 31 instructions a visit native and 56 with LLVM (iany
  against ifor), more than 0337 estimated (7 to 10): the prelude's
  list_any is not specialized at the closure literal, so each element
  is a call through a pointer of a function that is not inlined. The
  ADR's follow up (specialize the prelude's callback methods at a
  closure literal) is the next step for this gap.
- Checksums equal before and after on both backends.
