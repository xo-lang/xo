# CPU quota stalls: Xo's HTTP server under a 2 CPU limit (2026-11-14)

Summary tables behind decisions 0638 (step 2), 0640 and 0641, gathered
for the xolang.dev post on the stalls. Every figure is copied from those
decisions (knowledgebase/decisions/0638-public-runtime-checks-threads-metrics.md
"Step 2 results", 0640-root-helper-takes-worker-slot.md "Consequences" and
"Follow-up", 0641-monitor-polls-network.md "Results"); the raw logs are in
the measuring agent's notes.

Setup, every table: the linux/amd64 offload host (28 CPUs), a systemd
user scope with CPUQuota=200% (cpu.max 200000 100000, what
`docker --cpus=2` writes; nproc still 28), bench/perf/http's CRUD server,
its loadgen outside the scope, 10 s of load after 2 s of warmup. Go
1.26.11, whose GOMAXPROCS follows the quota (2).

## 4. The last throttle: tried and rejected

Decision 0641, the monitor doing the poller thread's fallback poll, 8
alternated pairs:

| | before | the monitor polls |
|---|---|---|
| poller thread CPU (ticks of 10 ms a run) | 2 | 0 |
| throttles (runs with any) | 2 (2 of 8) | 3 (2 of 8) |
| rps | 127.7k to 137.7k | 125.7k to 141.9k |

With no monitor at all (XO_DEFER_WAKE=0) 8 runs a side throttled 1 and
3 times: about one throttle of 0.1 to 0.5 ms in a quarter of the runs is
CFS's floor at saturation (quota handed to each CPU in 5 ms slices,
sched_cfs_bandwidth_slice_us 5000).
