# lock_slow spin rounds, 100 against 1600 (2026-11-01, Xo Lang 4)

The runtime's lock takes its first try inline, then spins in `lock_slow`
before a futex sleep. The change: 1600 rounds in place of 100, Linux only
(decision 0324, "Lock spin rounds"). This is not `XO_SPIN_ROUNDS`, the idle
worker's spin of spin-rounds-2026-10-22.md.

## HTTP CRUD bench, linux/arm64 first host, exclusive, 10 rounds, rotated order

bench/perf/http, 8222cbb7c against the same plus the change. No errors in any
run. Median (range).

| connections | rounds | req/s | p50 us | p99 us | p99.9 us | CPU us/req |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256 | 100 | 226,566 (222,456 to 227,329) | 1,168 (1,161 to 1,195) | 3,664 (3,624 to 3,747) | 5,494 (5,277 to 5,762) | 15.89 (15.84 to 16.18) |
| 256 | 1600 | 233,595 (231,572 to 236,291) | 1,089 (1,074 to 1,112) | 3,608 (3,535 to 3,740) | 5,656 (5,278 to 6,414) | 15.84 (15.66 to 15.98) |
| 32 | 100 | 219,612 (218,136 to 221,247) | 120 (119 to 121) | 711 (703 to 729) | 1,260 (1,226 to 1,348) | 13.61 (13.11 to 13.75) |
| 32 | 1600 | 222,908 (196,690 to 224,155) | 118 (117 to 121) | 712 (704 to 1,022) | 1,261 (1,155 to 3,031) | 13.43 (13.01 to 13.57) |

Against 100 rounds, medians: at 256, req/s +3.1%, p99 -1.5%, p99.9 +2.9% with
overlapping ranges; at 32, req/s +1.5%, p99 +0.1%, p99.9 +0.1%. The worst run
at 32 and 1600 rounds (196,690 req/s, p99.9 3,031 us) is one round of ten, and
its cause was not looked for; it is why that row's ranges are wide. The p99.9 of
+8.5% in an earlier 3 round run did not repeat. The owner's bar: under +8.5% with
ranges overlapping or close.
