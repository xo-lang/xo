more than LLVM's for the same Xo source, close what is in std, and send

## Where it goes

Profiles: valgrind's callgrind in Docker (linux/arm64; the client built
by a linux/arm64 xo cross built with the Go backend, both backends in
the container, the Go serve/ as the peer), 20 x25519only handshakes:
216.1M instructions on arm64, 142.5M on LLVM. The P-256 chain check is
most of both: about 151M on arm64 against 98M (nistec.mul4p,
sub_m4, pt_mul, add4p, sqr4p, sub4p, ...), and X25519 about 32M
against 20M.

- `nistec.sub_m4` (the final conditional subtraction of mul4p, sqr4p,
  add4p) was 31.4M: 785,000 calls of 40 instructions, a call on arm64
  (10 parameters, 7 call sites) where LLVM inlines it and uses an sbcs
  chain.
- `nistec.pt_mul` was 22.6M on arm64 against 5.2M: its constant time
  table selection read, masked, and wrote back all 27 words of a point
  (PS, sized for P-521) for each of the 16 entries in each of the 64
  windows, though P-256 points have 12 live words.
- `bigmod.mac_row` (RSA): each `cpu.adc(x, y, 0)` had its constant 0
  carry in hoisted into a register, so the emitter paired nothing:
  about 16 instructions a word against LLVM's 10.
- `x25519.fe64_mul`: 173 instructions against 101; the generated first
  row adds products to a literal 0, held in a register, which broke
  the adcs chain at each one.

## Std changes

- `nistec.sub_m4`: t - m as t + ~m + 1, one chain of cpu.adc (an adcs
  chain on arm64), the selects and stores written out with the mask
  that has passed ct.mask_bit's barrier (no ct.pick or st calls). Still
  a call on arm64 (seven sites is over the inliner's few sites limit;
  Xo lang 5 parked the inliner budget after DWARF).
- `nistec.pt_mul`: the 16 masks once a window, then for each of the
  3 n live words the OR of the 16 masked entries in registers and one
  store (unrolled). Still constant time: every entry is read for every
  window.

## Emitter findings (to Xo lang 5)

1. A constant 0 that reaches rt.u64_adc through a register (hoisted, or
   an inlined callee's block parameter) defeats const_int and same_arg.
2. Constant 0 operands of adcs held in a register instead of xzr, and x
   + 0 + carry as cset, cmp, adcs where LLVM has cinc.
3. sub_m4 not inlined (the inliner's block and few sites limits).
4. A wrapping add and cpu.carry of the same operands with a store
   between them not fused into adds (parked).

1 and 2 landed as a29d7947 (Xo lang 5): on arm64 rsa2048 0.946, tls12
0.951, x25519 0.953, p256 0.956, ed25519 0.961, p384 0.978 of before;
fe64_mul 173 to 148 instructions, mac_row 149 to 124.

## The fe64 generator's literal zeros (measured, not yet landed)

scripts/x25519fe64.py with chain() folding a literal 0 operand when the
carry in is 0 (no instruction, no carry out), checked in Python against
big integer arithmetic on 20,144 cases: on top of a29d7947, fe64_mul
148 to 138 instructions on arm64, and on arm64 x25519only 9.40M to
9.27M (-1.5%), x25519 10.62M to 10.41M (-2.0%), ed25519 9.92M to 9.72M
(-2.0%), tls12 11.08M to 10.81M (-2.4%); LLVM unchanged (it folded the
zeros already). Held back at Xo lang 5's request while a29d7947 was
gated against the current source; next.
