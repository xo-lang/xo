Status: Accepted (2026-10-07)

## Context
Remaining gaps from the v0.3 paper tests and harder tasks. ([planted and harder](../../bench/results/planted-and-harder-2026-10-07.md))

## Decision
- `http.stream` with `BodyWriter` for streaming responses; client follows up to 10 redirects; `parse_url` grammar stated.
- `Fs.write_only`; `create_temp` returns a root relative path; `walk` on a missing dir is `NotFound`.
- Fuller `Set` API.
- `FakeNet.fail` (persistent) vs `fail_next`; `MemFs.write` creates parents; `fail_next` globs; `testing.run_main`.
- `Semaphore` is a handle. Std enums are module qualified (`testing.FsOp`).
- Core 5.4 documents keeping `Mutex.with` changes while failing (return a `Result`, then `?`).
