# 0323: a 2-wide f64 SLP pass for the arm64 backend

Status: Accepted for stages 1 and 2 (owner, 2026-10-21, via the lead:
the Rust comparison's ranked fixes); stage 3 proposed

## Context

bench/perf/llvm nbody on native arm64 runs 2,890 M instructions (after
0322) against Rust's 1,410 M. Its inner loop is 43 instructions a pair of
bodies, about 25 of them scalar Float operations on (x, y, z) and (vx,
vy, vz) triples: three fsub, the dx*dx + dy*dy + dz*dz sum, three
updates of each velocity. Rust's build (LLVM's SLP vectorizer) does the
x and y lanes of each triple in one 128-bit NEON instruction (fsub.2d,
fmul.2d, ldr/str q), the z lane alone. Each lane of a NEON double
instruction rounds exactly as the scalar instruction does, so pairing
keeps decision 0287 (every Float operation rounded, no fusion, the same
result on every backend): no fmla, no reassociation.

## Decision (proposed)

A MIR pass, `slp_f64`, in compiler/arm64 (listed in passes.xo), after
instruction selection and before register allocation, in three stages.

1. A wide vreg class. G gains `wide: List[Bool]` beside `fp`: a wide
   vreg holds two doubles in a V register. Allocation gives it only
   caller saved V registers (v16 to v31 and v0 to v7, never v8 to v15,
   whose upper halves a callee does not keep), and the pass never makes
   a wide value live across a call (all upper halves are caller saved
   on AAPCS64, so it would need a 16 byte spill around every call). A
   spilled wide vreg gets a 16 byte, 16 aligned slot (ldr q / str q).
   The emitter names it `v<n>.2d` (or `q<n>` in a load or store).
2. Straight line pairing along an extended basic block (a chain of
   blocks, each the only successor of the last on its hot path, whose
   other exits go to cold blocks: the list updates' fault paths split
   nbody's pair body into b6, b7, b8, b9, b11, b12, ... at MIR time,
   merged only by layout; a lane a cold exit reads is extracted on that
   exit, the hot chain stays packed): seeds are pairs of fp loads
   from the same base at offsets k and k + 8 (`ldr d, [x, #k]`,
   `[x, #k+8]`, one `ldr q` when nothing between them stores to the
   base) and pairs of fp stores likewise; a pair grows through
   isomorphic operations whose operands are packs (fadd, fsub, fmul,
   fdiv, fsqrt, fneg, fabs: their `.2d` forms), and through a scalar
   used by both lanes (`fmul.2d v, v, v.d[0]` by element, or dup). A
   lane read by a scalar use is extracted (`mov d, v.d[1]`, lane 0 is
   the d register itself), a pack fed by scalars is built (`ins
   v.d[1], d`). A pack is kept only when its tree saves instructions
   after the extracts and inserts (a cost model counting MIR
   instructions; ties left scalar). Horizontal sums keep their order:
   (a + b) + c pairs (a, b) and adds lane 0 and lane 1 with faddp,
   whose IEEE addition is commutative, then adds c as before.
3. Loop carried packs: a pair of fp block parameters of a loop header
   whose incoming values are packs on every edge becomes one wide
   parameter (nbody's bs[i].vx, vy held across the j loop by
   hoist_fields). Phase 3 only after phase 2 measures.

Pass order: before hoist_consts and hoist_invariants (a pack of
loop invariant scalars, nbody's bs[i].x and y held by hoist_fields, is
built with ins once, hoisted out of the j loop by hoist_invariants) and
before cluster_mem (which would pair the same loads into ldp).

Stage 1 as built (2026-10-21): G.wide, new_wide, $q/$v template names
(the emitter's operand scan reads them as $w), MLoc.wide with 128 bit
moves, q_mem (ldr/str q, ldur/stur, or through x17), wide reloads and
spill stores, regalloc (no coalescing across the class, caller saved
candidates only, two spill slots).

Not paired: Float32 (later, `.4s`), Int lanes, anything across a call,
anything under XO_PASSES=-slp_f64 (the build stays correct), and the
x86_64 retarget (SSE2 `addpd` is a separate decision; the pass checks
g.x86).

## Consequences (to measure per stage)

- Instructions on bench/perf/llvm nbody (native arm64), the perf suite,
  xo's own __text (native and LLVM, split 12, no LTO), and a cold self
  build's time (the pass's cost).
- Correctness: every golden on arm64 (XO_INLINE_STRICT=1), progen's
  Float programs (TestArithProperty Float paths), and a new golden with
  pairs at every edge (NaN, -0.0, infinities, subnormals, a lane read
  as a scalar, a store between the two loads of a pair, a call between
  def and use) whose output equals the Go and LLVM backends' bit for
  bit.
- The register allocator's verifier and the MIR dumps (decision 0322's
  XO_DUMP_FN) learn the wide class.

## Alternatives

- Fused multiply add (fmadd): ruled out by decision 0287.
- Leaving vectorizing to the LLVM backend: it already does it when the
  list facts hold (decision 0322 and the LLVM list facts); native is the
  default backend (decision 0248), so the gap stays where most programs
  run.
- A loop vectorizer (4 or more lanes across iterations): nbody's
  parallelism is across fields, not iterations; a later decision.

## Stage 2 as built (2026-10-21)

compiler/arm64 slp.xo, between select_compares and hoist_consts:
- Chains: hot blocks each the only successor of the last, by the
  terminators' targets (MBlock.succs and preds are made later).
- Seeds: every pair of Float loads of one base 8 apart whose later load
  may move up (only stores to the same base outside its 8 bytes between),
  scored by the pairs of the same operation among the lanes' users, taken
  best first, one pair per load, none that scores 0.
- Growth to a fixed point with no builds first, then with builds (zip1 of
  two scalars, one instruction that hoist_invariants moves out of a loop
  when both are invariant); an operation found later for lanes a build
  held replaces the build in place.
- Store pairs (str q) where nothing between reads or writes the 16 bytes
  (load pack members excepted: they move to their earlier load), faddp
  for a sum of a pack's two lanes, fmul by element for a scalar both
  lanes multiply by, dup otherwise.
- A decision per group of packs that feed each other: one saved per
  pack and store pair, one per extract (dup dN, vM.d[i]), per build from
  a scalar defined in the chain, per dup; a group whose lane is read
  outside the chain or before its pack is left scalar.
- compiler/macho encodes fadd/fsub/fmul/fdiv/fsqrt .2d, fmul by element,
  faddp, dup (vector and scalar from a lane), ins, zip1 (words checked
  against the system assembler).
- XO_DUMP_FN=NAME also writes xo-ir-slp-NAME.txt (each chain's packs and
  each group's decision).
Results (Mac arm64 instructions): bench/perf/llvm nbody 2,890 to 2,575 M
(-10.9%), its inner loop 43 to 33 instructions; output identical; golden
slp_f64 prints the bits of every result (rounding sensitive finite
values, NaN, -0.0, infinities, subnormals, a lane read as a scalar, a
store between a pair's loads, a call between pairs, faddp) and is the
same on Go, LLVM, arm64 with and without the pass.
Gate: every arm64 golden with XO_INLINE_STRICT=1 (and TestGoldenAsm,
TestAmd64PassesFinish), the arm64 and macho unit tests, TestPassesListed,
xo dev codegen, the linux/arm64 Docker goldens slp_f64, lists, maps,
interp, json_text, and the fixed point. bench/perf/llvm natively, with
and without the pass (same xo): nbody 2,900 to 2,578 M (-11.1%); collatz,
fib, interp, sieve, sort, strings within 0.3%. xo itself: native __text
51,426,968 either way (no pairs in the compiler); a cold self build 172.8
against 167.9 s of CPU (to profile: the pass's cost, or noise).
Stage 3 (loop carried packs, nbody's bs[i].vx and vy across the j loop)
and Float32 lanes remain.

2026-10-25: the golden's bits() clears a NaN's sign bit
(`& 9223372036854775807`). Every NaN in it is made by an invalid
