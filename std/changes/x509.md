## 2026-10-29: feature: std/x509, certificate parsing and chain verification

- Kind: feature
- Commit: this commit
- Found by: milestone 0.35.0 S5 (public x509 from the parser and verifier TLS already has)
- Go reference: crypto/x509 (ParseCertificate, Certificate.Verify, VerifyHostname, CheckSignatureFrom, SystemCertPool's sources), crypto/x509/pkix (Name, Name.String) (go1.26.1)
- Test: internal/stdtest/testdata/corpus/x509 (scripts/stdcorpus x509.go: every certificate of Go's crypto/x509 and crypto/tls testdata, a fifth of the NIST PKITS certificates, verify_test.go's certificates and truncated and bit-flipped copies, each field and error text against Go's ParseCertificate; verify_test.go's verifyTests rows with roots against Go's Verify chains and errors; Name escapes) on go, arm64 and LLVM; bench/perf/x509 twin

New module std/x509 (spec std.md 41): `parse(der)` and `parse_pem(pem)`
give a `Certificate` (raw DER, version, serial, Go's signature algorithm
name, issuer and subject `Name`s with Go's `display()` and every
`attributes()`, validity, `crypto.PublicKey`, DNS names, IP addresses,
e-mail addresses, URIs, basic constraints, key usages, extended key
usages, key ids and every extension). `Certificate.verify(opts)` is Go's
Verify with explicit roots, intermediates, DNS name, time and key
usages, returning every chain or Go's error text; `verify_hostname`,
`check_signature_from`, and `system_roots(fs)`, the roots std/tls
checks servers against (on macOS the system's anchor certificates). The
native backends wrap the TLS stack's parser and verifier
(std/native/llrt/x509cert.xo, x509verify.xo), which now also keep every
extension, the Go backend Go's crypto/x509.

bench/perf/x509 against Go's crypto/x509 (quiet `xo bench --ab`, 15
rounds, Go as A, wall): parse_pem of a three certificate chain 1.183,
verify 1.002. The PEM scan (std/native/llrt/tlsname.xo find_at, shared
with std/tls and std/http's PEM roots and keys) compares a marker's first
byte before the rest: parse_pem 0.865 of the parent's wall.
